# Smart Meal Planner recipes: licence

recipes.json holds recipes from the [Wikibooks Cookbook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook), written by its
contributors and licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each recipe's `sourceURL` links to the exact page
revision it was taken from, and `authorsURL` to the page history listing its authors.

Adapted for Smart Meal Planner: amounts converted to grams and ingredients named from a standard list. Nutrition values per 100 g come from USDA FoodData Central (public domain, CC0 1.0); prices are the
project's own estimates. These adapted recipes are shared under the same licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Photos: where a recipe has `imageURL`, the photo is from Wikimedia Commons under the licence in `imageLicense`, by
`imageAuthor`; see `imageSourceURL` for its details.
