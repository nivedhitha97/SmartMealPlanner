# Privacy policy

Smart Meal Planner · effective 26 October 2026

Smart Meal Planner is a personal, non-commercial project by Nivedhitha Parthasarathy ("I"). This policy explains what
happens to your information when you use the app. In short: **everything you enter stays on your phone. I don't
receive, collect or sell any of it.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone

Your preferences (diet, cuisines, allergies, dislikes, budget, postcode, number of people), your meal routine, your
weekly plans, your grocery list, the deals you add, your spending overview and your settings are saved only on your
phone. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. Allergies and dietary choices can say something about your health;
they never leave your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

## What the app downloads, and who can see that

To work, the app downloads data. Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address and basic device information
(such as the iOS version) to the service that answers. No information you entered is sent with them.

- **Offers, prices and recipes** come from the project's public feed on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Recipe photos** load from Wikimedia (upload.wikimedia.org). See the
  [Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).
- **Your postcode** is looked up with Apple Maps to find your area and the supermarkets near you; Apple receives the
  postcode for that search. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What happens on your phone only

- **Dish ideas**: where Apple Intelligence is on, Apple's on-device model suggests dishes that suit your preferences.
  This runs entirely on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Offer alerts** are local notifications scheduled by the phone; no server is involved.

## Links you open

Buttons such as "Original" (Wikibooks), "Watch" (a YouTube search), "Open Bonus" (Albert Heijn) and "Licence" open
those websites or apps. What they collect is covered by their own privacy policies.

## Your rights

Because I don't receive or store any of your personal data, there is nothing I hold about you to access, correct or
delete; your data is on your phone and under your control. Under the GDPR you may also lodge a complaint with your
data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Children

The app is not aimed at children under 16.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version appears in the app and on GitHub with a new date. Questions: open an issue at
[github.com/nivedhitha97/SmartMealPlanner/issues](https://github.com/nivedhitha97/SmartMealPlanner/issues).
