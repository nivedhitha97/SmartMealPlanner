# Data sources and licences

Smart Meal Planner uses only data that may be freely used and shared. No supermarket website is read.

## Recipes: Wikibooks Cookbook

The recipes are from the [Wikibooks Cookbook](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook), written by its contributors and
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Each recipe page in the app credits
its authors, links to the exact revision it was taken from and to its page history, and says what was changed: amounts
converted to grams and ingredients named from a standard list. The adapted recipes are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 too,
in the project's feed (recipes.json).

## Recipe photos: Wikimedia Commons

Photos are from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). Only photos in the public domain or under a
Creative Commons licence (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA) are used, each credited on its recipe page with its author, licence
and source.

## Nutrition: USDA FoodData Central

Nutrition values and portion weights come from [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/) (SR Legacy), which is
in the public domain ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).

## Offers and prices: Open Prices

Offers and shelf prices are reported by shoppers on [Open Prices](https://prices.openfoodfacts.org/).
Price data © Open Food Facts contributors, available under the
[Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The project's feed built from it is
shared under the ODbL too. Ingredient prices that aren't from Open Prices are the project's own estimates.

## Maps: Apple

Postcode lookups and nearby supermarkets use Apple Maps (MapKit).

## Dish ideas: Apple Intelligence

Dish names are suggested by Apple's on-device model (Foundation Models), only as search ideas. The model never writes or
changes a recipe.
